      Conditions to keep the incentive
      • Management and controlManaged and controlled in GMC from year two. Representatives present in GMC or Bhutan at least 30 days a year; 60 days with an employment pass for managers at US$25M or more.
      • Local hiringBy year three, at least two Bhutanese resident in GMC in front- or middle-office roles. Three for managers at US$25M or more.
      • Local spendMore than US$50,000 a year in GMC, including salaries and office rent. US$100,000 for managers at US$25M or more.
      • BankingAn account with a GMC-licensed bank. DK Bank offers nine currencies, Fedwire access, stablecoin transfers and custody.
      • ReportingResults against the conditions are reported in the annual corporate tax return. Missing them two years running ends the incentive, with no clawback.

      No foreign exchange controls outside Bhutanese ngultrum. No withholding tax on dividends and no capital gains tax. Investors face no profile restrictions. A Singapore double tax agreement is signed and in ratification.

      Questions

      What families and managers ask first.

      Does a single-family office need a licence?

      No. It notifies GFSO of its activities. It needs approval only if a multi-family office provides its trustee services.

      When must we meet the US$10M minimum?

      Only at the point of applying. Legally enforceable committed capital counts toward assets under management.

      What can a family office or fund invest in?

      Anything. The exemption covers designated investments, a broad list including equities, bonds, digital assets, commodities and property anywhere.

      Which entity forms are available?

      Companies, variable capital companies, partnerships and trusts. Trusts are recognised under Singapore and English common law.

      What if we miss a condition one year?

      The incentive continues that year. It ends only after two consecutive years of non-compliance, and earlier relief is not clawed back.

      Are you the regulator or a service provider?

      Neither. We are an independent onboarding desk. Filings go through GMC's empanelled Corporate Service Providers; decisions rest with GFSO and the GMC tax office.
